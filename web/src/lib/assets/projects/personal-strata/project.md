This site. A portfolio as an archaeological dig seen by a site survey: the projects stand on a real landscape, drawn live in the browser, their names tied to the ground by survey lines.

The ground is New Zealand lidar at half scale, coloured from aerial photographs. The projects' objects are stamped into it in Houdini, where the sun's shadows are baked.

In the browser, raw WebGL2: the terrain is raymarched and cached, then the atmosphere is drawn every frame: fog, drifting mist, light shafts through it, water. Even the labels cast shadows into the shafts. It adapts itself to cheap phones.
