A tree and root growth simulation for offline rendering (advertising, luxury goods), grown on the graphics card in Houdini with OpenCL.

It starts from space colonization (Runions, 2007): a seed grows through a cloud of attractor points. On top of it, rules from botany, each from its source: vigor and apical dominance, phyllotaxis at the golden angle, Leonardo's rule for branch angles, Murray's law for thickness, reaction wood, McMahon's limit on length. Obstacles, surfaces, density and light come in as volumes.

About 800,000 branch nodes, at around a second per frame.
