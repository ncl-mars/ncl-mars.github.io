An interactive 3D map of the Mont Blanc area for the Compagnie du Mont-Blanc, in summer and in winter, embedded in its website.

The landscape is modelled in Blender and drawn in the browser with Three.js, the interface in Svelte. Clouds open onto the map, and the camera flies from one area to the next; the seasons swap the map's colours, its fog and its light.

Built for speed: the best frame rate and the shortest loading time, inside someone else's page, and no work for the graphics card while nobody is looking.
