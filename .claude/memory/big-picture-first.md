The user wants the big picture first: a simple graph of the full flow and its stages (inputs, steps, outputs, what drives what), at the top of a plan or doc, and in chat when proposing a pipeline. The details come after, as the material behind each box.

**Why:** 2026-11-06, the fossils plan. After pages of stages and tables: "I don't understand your proposal, can you make a clear simple graph?", then "why isn't the big picture in the fossile doc, the big picture is all that matter !", and "I like to see the big picture and the full flow and stages".

**How to apply:** when proposing or documenting a pipeline or a plan, start with a compact diagram (a monospace graph in a code block) of the whole flow and a few lines on what drives what, then the details. Keep the diagram in the doc itself, at the top, and update it whenever the plan changes. Example: the Big picture section of `doc/fossils.md`.
