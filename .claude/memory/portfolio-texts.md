The project cards' texts (`web/src/lib/assets/projects/*/project.md`) are for a jury and visitors: short, simple sentences, carried by the user's own pertinent keywords (for Lotchi: "interactive ID map reprojection", "scan colour reprojection", "interactive GPU rasterization", "2.5D", "Copernicus"). Never paraphrase his internal notes into node or block names ("data-driven UV layout into UDIMs", "rasterization of the ID maps and their passes"): he called that "variable names". Never upgrade his role: at Lotchi he was hired as a senior technical artist, not lead ("surtout pas"). Tell his story as a progression in his own framing ("I started… Then… Finally…"), not as a count ("I worked with Lotchi three times" was "so bad").

**Why:** 2026-11-13, writing the Lotchi card the day the portfolio had to go live for a CNRS application; each of those drafts angered him.

**How to apply:** for each project, ask for (or reuse) his keywords and facts; build the text around them in plain words; keep only facts he stated or that his project docs state; never public wording that could read as criticism of a colleague's tool. Related: [[verify-facts]].
